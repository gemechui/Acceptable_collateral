    Vehicle age is the valuation year minus the year of make. A vehicle exactly 1, 3 or 5 years old falls in the older band, and exactly 10 years is still 30%.

    The 25% limit means vehicles can be at most one third of the building acceptable value. Any vehicle value above that is not counted in the total eligible collateral.

    Net collateral available is the total eligible collateral less the existing loan. Leave the existing loan empty if there is none.
